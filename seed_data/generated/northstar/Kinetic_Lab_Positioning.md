Most running shoes are built for a size chart. Kinetic Lab builds for you. Scan your feet with the phone you already own, choose your look, and we make a pair around your arch, width and stride. No showroom, no guessing between a 9 and a 9.5, no compromise.

- Built to order, one pair at a time

- Designed in our studio and made in our own micro factory

## How it works

### 1. Scan

Open the Kinetic Lab app and follow the guided scan. Place each foot on a sheet of paper, move your phone slowly around it, and the app captures length, width, arch height and instep volume in about two minutes. We check the scan for quality and ask for a redo if anything looks off.

### 2. Build

Pick a base model, a colorway and a midsole firmness. Our software turns your scan into a last, adjusts the insole contour and sends the file to the micro factory. Each pair is cut, bonded and finished by a technician rather than a mass production line.

### 3. Deliver

Kinetic Lab builds every pair to order from a phone foot scan and promises delivery in 14 to 21 days. You can follow your pair through cutting, assembly and quality check in the app, and you will receive a tracking link as soon as it ships.

## Pricing

Kinetic Lab prices its custom runner at $210, and charges $35 for a rescan fitting. If your first pair does not feel right, one rescan is always included in the first 30 days of your fit guarantee. Our trainer starts at a lower price than the custom runner, and socks and replacement insoles are available at checkout. We do not run permanent sales because every pair is made for one person.

## Our story

Kinetic Lab began in a garage workshop, when two friends who ran together grew tired of buying shoes that pinched on one side and slipped on the other. They taught themselves last making, wrote the first scanning software on weekends, and built twenty pairs for their running club. Today a small team of fit scientists, designers and technicians makes every pair in our own micro factory, and each customer still gets a human reply when they write to us.

## Frequently asked questions

### How accurate is a phone scan?

The scan captures the measurements that matter for last design, and our software compares it against thousands of reference feet. If a measurement looks unusual we ask you to scan again before we cut any material.

### What if my pair does not fit?

Tell us within 30 days. We will arrange a rescan fitting and rebuild the pair where we can. Our fit team reviews every request personally.

### Can I choose any colour?

Our configurator offers a curated palette so that every combination is one we are proud of. Seasonal colours rotate in small runs.

### Do you ship outside the United States?

Not yet. We currently deliver to all 50 states and are testing Canada in the coming year.

## Sustainability

Because we only build what has been ordered, we hold almost no unsold stock and send no overruns to discount channels. Small batch production lets us buy materials in the quantities we need, and offcuts from upper panels are collected and reused in insole layers where possible.

We publish the country of origin of every material and the recycled content of each midsole. Our packaging is a single reusable mailer, and customers can send worn pairs back to our micro factory for resoling rather than replacing them.
