# Pace & Co. Positioning (web snapshot)

Fictional data created for the MarketSignal demo.

Snapshot captured August 2026 from the Pace & Co. public website. Navigation and images removed.

## Hero

Good gear should not cost a month of rent. Pace & Co. makes athleisure for people who move through the day, from the studio to the street to the couch. New styles drop every two weeks, so there is always something fresh, and our core leggings are just $38.

- New drops every two weeks
- Core leggings at $38
- Free returns and no fuss exchanges

## The range

Our range covers leggings and shorts, sports bras, tees and tanks, hoodies and joggers, and a small selection of everyday trainers. Each drop is a focused edit of around twenty styles in a seasonal colour story. When a style sells out, it is gone, and we replace it with something new instead of restocking last month's hit.

Our fabrics are soft, stretchy and made to survive a lot of washes. Leggings are available in four lengths and sizes from XXS to 4X, and we publish the same size chart across every style so that one size works across the range.

## Our price promise

We buy in volume, keep our designs simple and skip the expensive ad campaigns, and we pass the savings to you. Our promise is that our core leggings stay at $38 for the whole season, even when cotton prices move. If a style you bought drops in price within two weeks, we will credit you the difference.

## Returns made easy

Pace & Co. offers free returns within 60 days and says 70% of its range is priced under $50. Start your return in the app, drop the parcel at a partner location or leave it with your delivery driver, and you will see your refund within a few days of it reaching us. Items must be unwashed with tags attached, apart from trainers, which can be returned after a short test.

## Community and creators

Pace & Co. grew through the people who wear it. Our Creator Crew programme invites everyday athletes, dance teachers, runners and gym regulars to share outfits and honest reviews with their followers. Members receive early access to each drop, a unique discount code and a commission on sales.

We also run weekly community challenges, from a thirty day step goal to a studio class passport, with prizes in gift credit. Every month we feature a customer in a campaign, so the faces in our shop are the ones that are in our community.

## Find us in store

You can try Pace & Co. in person inside partner retailers. Our store in store shops are small, bright corners in selected department and sports stores, stocked with the latest drop and our core essentials. Look for the Pace & Co. fixtures in over a thousand doors across the country, and use the store finder to locate your nearest one.

Store in store shops stock core leggings, tees and sports bras in every size, plus a rotating wall of the newest drops. Online exclusives can be ordered in store and sent to your home.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often do new styles arrive?

Every two weeks, usually on a Thursday. Sign up for alerts in the app and you will see each drop before it goes live on the site.

### Are your leggings squat proof?

Our core leggings are tested for opacity and stretch, and we show the results on each product page. If you are not satisfied with the fit, our return policy covers you.

### Can I customize or personalize my order?

Not at the moment. We keep every style simple so that we can drop something new every fortnight and hold our prices down. Tell us what you want to see in the next drop through the app.

### How do I join Creator Crew?

Apply through the community page. We look for authentic voices of every size and level, not a particular follower count.

### Do you ship internationally?

We ship within the United States and are exploring Canada. Partner retailer shops are currently available only in the United States.
