## Our mission

Vantage Athletic describes its mission as engineering speed for athletes who compete, and its homepage leads with race results rather than lifestyle imagery. That is why our design studio is staffed by biomechanists, materials scientists and former collegiate coaches, and why every prototype is lab tested and then handed to athletes before a single pair reaches a shelf.

Performance is not a slogan for us. It is the thing we measure, publish and defend. When a result moves, we want you to be able to trace it back to a decision we made in the lab.

## Product families

### Racing

Our racing line is anchored by the flagship racer, a carbon plated shoe with a high rebound foam stack and a rocker geometry tuned for marathon pace. Alongside it sit a lighter track spike and a road racer for tempo days. Vantage Athletic offers personalization only as a choice of 12 preset colorways on its flagship racer. We keep the engineering identical for every runner, because the plate, the foam and the geometry are tuned together and we do not want a custom tweak to undo that work.

### Training

The training family covers daily trainers, long run cruisers and strength session shoes, plus a full apparel range in moisture managing fabrics. Daily trainers use a durable engineered mesh upper with reinforced toe and flex zones, and we publish a wear window for every model so that you know how many miles to expect before a replacement.

### Recovery

After the work comes the rebuild. Our recovery range includes cushioned slides, compression sleeves and a percussion tool that was developed with team physiotherapists. Each item is rated for the session it follows, from an easy shakeout to a double header weekend.

## Fit Lab

Fit Lab is our in store gait and fit service. A trained technician films your stride on a pressure plate, measures foot volume and arch behaviour, and recommends a model and size from our range. Sessions take about ten minutes, are free, and need no purchase. You can book a slot through the store locator, and results are saved to your Vantage profile so that a future pair can be matched to the same fit data.

Fit Lab recommendations are made from our existing range. We believe a correct size in the right model beats a bespoke shoe for nearly every runner, and it keeps delivery to the same day you walk out of the store.

## Sustainability

Our sustainability page sets out three commitments. First, by 2028 we aim for half of our footwear midsoles to include recycled or bio based foam. Second, we publish the country of manufacture and the main material composition for every product. Third, we run a repair and resole programme for our racing and training shoes, so that a worn outsole does not mean a discarded pair.

We report progress once a year and explain where we missed a target. Performance comes first, but a shoe that lasts longer is also a better performing shoe.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I choose between the racer and a daily trainer?

Use the racer for goal races and key workouts, and a daily trainer for the bulk of your mileage. The racer is built for speed rather than durability, so it will wear faster than a trainer.

### What does Fit Lab cost?

Nothing. A session is free in every participating store, and you are under no obligation to buy.

### Can I return shoes I have run in?

Yes. You have 30 days to return footwear that has been worn on short test runs, provided it is in sellable condition.

### Do you offer student or club pricing?

Collegiate and club programmes can apply for team pricing through the team sales page. Individual athletes can join the Vantage membership for early access to race releases.
