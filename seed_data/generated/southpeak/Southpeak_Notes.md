## Purpose

These are working notes from the brand refresh team, written after the September planning session. They capture where we are, what has been decided, what is still open and who owns the next steps. They are not a finished plan and will change as we learn more from the pilots and from the second half results.

## Where we are

The refresh rests on a simple idea: customers like Southpeak gear on the trail, but the experience around the gear is inconsistent. The spring survey and the June interviews point to the same cluster of issues: fit that changes between boots, trail runners and packs, uncertainty about how long gear should last, a wish for plainer information about materials, and delivery that does not always meet expectations. The brand strategy and the Q2 board deck both treat these as the priorities.

Sales are tracking close to the board plan, and the full year latest estimate stays at $284.0 million. The Ridgeline shell jacket remains the product that best expresses the refresh, and early patch orders from Basecamp Rewards members suggest that small personal touches are welcome.

## Decisions made

- Fit comes first. A single fit standard will be applied from the spring 2027 season, and the size guide will be redesigned around comparisons with gear customers already own.
- Repair is part of the brand. We will expand resole and zip repair, and publish expected service life for the top products.
- Sustainability information will be specific. Product pages will show recycled content and country of manufacture; general claims will be removed.
- Patches stay a small test. We will not commit to a wider personalization program until we have seen how patches affect delivery time and repeat purchase.
- Community gets its own line in the budget, starting with stewardship days and outfitter partner events.

## Delivery

Delivery generated the longest discussion. Operations argued that a reliable date at checkout is more valuable than a faster date that is sometimes missed, and the team agreed to treat accuracy as the first step. Southpeak will pilot a 48 hour delivery promise in the Denver and Salt Lake City markets starting in March 2027. The pilot will be assessed on whether customers who receive the faster option order again at a higher rate, and on what it costs per order.

We also agreed that any personalization feature must not lengthen the delivery promise. If a patch cannot be applied inside the promised window, it should ship separately, and the product page should say so before checkout.

## Open questions

- How large a premium, if any, will members accept for patches, and does it vary by segment?
- Should the fit standard be applied retroactively to existing styles or only at their next revision?
- Can outfitter partners take part in the delivery pilot, or should it start with direct channels only?
- What is the cost of a repair program at scale, and how much of it can be recovered through margin on retained customers?
- Which sustainability measures are credible and verifiable enough to publish on every product page?
- Do Family Campers need a separate size guide, since children's sizing varies with growth?

## Risks

The refresh depends on product teams sharing a fit standard, and the autumn calendar is already full. We will need executive sponsorship to avoid delays. There is also a risk of overpromising on sustainability: an inaccurate claim would undo trust faster than silence. Legal review will be built into the process, and every claim will have a named owner who can provide evidence.

A final risk is the cost of faster delivery. If the pilot is expensive and does not change repeat behavior, we will stop it and put the money into accuracy and tracking instead.

## Next steps

- Product: finalize the fit measurement standard and share a draft with the design teams in October.
- Operations: confirm the pilot design and carrier terms for the delivery promise.
- Brand: draft product page templates for service life and materials information.
- Rewards team: plan the next patch designs and a points scheme for stewardship days.
- Insights: repeat a short customer survey in spring 2027 to measure change against the 2026 baseline.
- Finance: prepare cost estimates for each FY27 ask in time for the next board meeting.

We will review these notes at the next weekly meeting and update the owner list. Anything that changes a decision above should be flagged to the brand lead before it is shared more widely.
